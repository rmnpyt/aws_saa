## Final Week Preparation

### 5-7 Days Before
- Take a full 65-question practice exam under timed conditions
- Review all wrong answers — understand the WHY, not just the right answer
- Don't try to learn new services this late

### 2-3 Days Before
- Review your personal "weak areas" — the categories where you consistently get wrong answers
- Review the domain summaries (security, resilience, performance, cost)
- Do a final 25-question practice set focusing on your weak areas

### Day Before
- Light review only — don't cram
- Review the Quick Reference lesson (Top 50 Services)
- Confirm your exam booking, testing center location/online proctored setup
- Prepare required ID documents
- Get good sleep — this matters more than last-minute studying

## Exam Registration

**Schedule at**: pearsonvue.com/aws

**Options:**
- **Testing center**: In-person at a Pearson VUE test center near you. Most consistent experience.
- **OnVUE (online proctored)**: Take it from home/office. Requirements: quiet room, no one else present, working webcam and microphone.

**Required ID**: Government-issued photo ID matching your exact registration name.

**Reschedule policy**: Free rescheduling up to 24 hours before the exam. After that, you forfeit the fee.

## During the Exam

### At the Testing Center
- Arrive 15-30 minutes early
- Bring your ID
- No phones, watches, or notes allowed in the exam room
- You'll be given scratch paper/whiteboard and marker

### Online Proctored
- Test your environment at home beforehand (Pearson provides a system check tool)
- Clear your desk completely
- Close all applications
- Have your ID ready to show the camera
- The proctor may ask you to show your room

## Exam Interface

- Navigate between questions freely using Next/Previous
- **Flag button**: Mark questions for review (yellow flag icon)
- **Review screen**: See all questions and which are flagged at any time
- You can review flagged questions at the end

## The Last-Minute Mental Checklist

As you start each question, ask:
1. **What is the question actually asking?** (HA, security, cost, performance?)
2. **What are the constraints?** (specific services, scale, budget, existing infrastructure?)
3. **Which answers can I eliminate immediately?**
4. **Between the remaining 2: which one better fits all requirements?**

## After the Exam

**Provisional result**: You'll see "Pass" or "Fail" on the screen immediately after submitting.

**Official score**: Delivered to your AWS Training account within 5 business days. Includes your overall score (100-1000) and per-domain performance.

**If you pass**: Certification is valid for 3 years. Download your certificate from credly.com (the digital badge service AWS uses).

**If you don't pass**: You can retake after 14 days. You must pay the exam fee again. The official score report shows which domains need improvement — use this to focus your re-studying.

## Score Interpretation

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| <600 | Need significant additional study (all domains) |
| 600-719 | Close! Review weak domains, retake within a month |
| 720-799 | Pass — solid foundation |
| 800-899 | Strong result — good understanding |
| 900-1000 | Excellent — near perfect |

## Last Day Mental State

Remember: The SAA-C03 exam tests whether you understand AWS architecture principles, not whether you've memorized every service's pricing down to the cent. If you've worked through this course and done practice exams:

- You understand when to use which service
- You know the key tradeoffs (stateful vs. stateless, HA vs. cost, etc.)
- You can eliminate wrong answers systematically

Trust your preparation. Read each question carefully. Take your time.

## Good luck! 🎯

The SAA certification is recognized globally and demonstrates a solid foundation in cloud architecture. The work you've put into this course will serve you well not just in the exam but in your actual AWS work.
